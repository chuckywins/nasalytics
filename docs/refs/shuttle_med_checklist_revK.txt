IMMEDIATE LIFE SUPPORT

BREATHING DIFFICULTY

        If difficulty develops during eating and person can
         talk/cough/whisper:
            1. No action but continue to monitor

        If difficulty develops during eating and person unable to
         breathe:
            2. Go to CHOKING, 1-4 >>

        If difficulty develops after exposure to dust/chemicals and
         person has wheezing/shortness of breath:
            3. Remove from exposure to chemical/irritant
            4. Protect others from exposure
